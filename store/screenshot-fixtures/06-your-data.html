Watchwise can’t open its encrypted local data.

The encryption key or stored records are unavailable or damaged. Watchwise has stopped local tracking to protect your privacy.

Resetting permanently deletes all Watchwise viewing history and settings in this Chrome profile. It creates a fresh encrypted store with tracking off.

Private by default

Understand your viewing without recording your screen.

Watchwise needs access to HTTPS pages so it can detect visible HTML5 video elements across sites and frames. It records only title, URL, site, playback position, duration, speed, state and time after you opt in.

  • No pixels, audio, captions, arbitrary page-body text or cookies
  • Titles come only from media attributes, Media Session, page title metadata and, on Netflix /watch/ pages, exact player-title metadata
  • History stays in this browser’s IndexedDB
  • Local records are AES-256-GCM encrypted before storage; the non-exportable key stays in this Chrome profile
